# Whole-book reader-value synthesis v2

Produce one evidence-backed whole-book reader-value synthesis from the supplied
verified claim cards. The task is not a chapter summary, quotation list, topic
pool, or isolated concept. Identify one target reader, one central life tension,
the change in understanding from before to after reading, practical value,
limits, and why the original book remains worth reading.

Return only the required structured fields. Provide exactly two or three
progressive clusters: begin with `problem`, then progress through `reframe`,
`application`, and optionally `boundary` without repeating or reversing roles.
Each cluster must cite only supplied claim IDs and its `chapter_regions` must
exactly match those claim IDs' supplied regions. Do not invent regions or claim
IDs. If fewer than three source regions exist, cover every available region and
state the structural limitation in `coverage_exception`; otherwise leave that
field null.

Write a synthesis rather than direct attribution. Do not use formulas such as
“书中说”, “书里说”, “作者说”, “作者认为”, “这本书告诉我们”, or “这本书证明”.
Do not promise outcomes or use guarantee or therapy language.
